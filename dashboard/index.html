⚡ Core Findings at a Glance

• Objective Pressure Definition: Pressure is defined purely through pre-ball win-probability leverage ($LI$), measuring counterfactual outcome swing potential. It eliminates look-ahead bias and static heuristics.

• Leverage-Neutral Currency: Normalising $WPAA$ by $LI$ yields a universal currency ($V = WPAA/LI$) that allows fair comparisons across powerplay, middle and death overs.

• Clutch Gap ($G = P - N$): Disentangles players who truly elevate their output in high-leverage moments from stars who flat-track in uncompetitive overs.

⚙️ The 5-Layer Statistical Engine

Layer 1: Pre-Ball Leverage Index (LI)
Trained out-of-fold HistGradientBoosting models with monotonic constraints predict pre-ball chase equity. In Innings 2, ROC AUC is 0.8935 and Log Loss is 0.4096. Swing potential on delivery t is max counterfactual WP minus min counterfactual WP. Normalised by mean swing, LI has sample mean 1.000. Deliveries with LI ≥ 1.34 define High and Extreme pressure (top 25%).

Layer 2: Leverage-Neutral Performance Currency (V = WPAA / LI)
A 7-class multiclass expectation model evaluates expected WP shifts. WPAA = observed ΔWP minus expected ΔWP. Dividing by LI scales execution into neutral skill units comparable across all phases.

Layer 3: Simultaneous Regularised Ridge Regression
Disentangles batter skill from opponent bowler difficulty: V_i = β_striker - γ_bowler. Tuned at α = 100.0 separately on pressure (P) and normal (N) deliveries. Verified against Empirical Bayes shrinkage (r > 0.99).

Layer 4: Clutch Elevation Gap (G) & Consistency (C)
Clutch Gap G = P - N rewards players who elevate under pressure. Consistency C = z(H) - 0.5 * z(σ) combines Beta-binomial shrunk match hit rate and shrunk match variance.

Layer 5: 400 Bootstrap Consensus Replicates
Composite score S = 0.50 * z(P) + 0.20 * z(G) + 0.30 * z(C). 400 bootstrap iterations perturb match samples, pressure thresholds (15% to 30%), and weights (+/-0.05). Final ranks are determined by consensus Borda score.

🔍 The Superstar Paradox: Bumrah, Narine & Klaasen

Why do legendary stars like Jasprit Bumrah, Sunil Narine and Heinrich Klaasen not appear in the top 5?

Jasprit Bumrah (Bowler Rank 15)
Pressure P: +0.055 | Normal N: +0.249 | Clutch Gap G: -0.194
Bumrah's pressure rating is elite (+0.055), but his normal baseline rating is a generational +0.249. In high-pressure situations, opposing batters shut down and tap him for singles, taking zero boundary risks. Because Bumrah operates at a ceiling in ordinary play, his performance does not show positive differential elevation under pressure ($G = -0.194$). Our model rewards players who elevate their output when stakes rise.
Sunil Narine (Bowler Rank 26)
Pressure P: +0.041 | Normal N: +0.182 | Clutch Gap G: -0.141
Similar to Bumrah, opposition batters play Narine with extreme defensive caution under leverage. While his baseline suppression is world-class, his lack of high-leverage wicket spikes prevents him from elevating his team's win probability beyond his already high baseline expectation.
Heinrich Klaasen (Batter Rank 12)
Pressure P: +0.037 | Normal N: +0.178 | Clutch Gap G: -0.141
Klaasen produces massive boundary striking in standard overs ($N = 0.178$). In extreme pressure situations, opposing captains specifically hold back their premier death pacers to target him with wide yorkers and short balls, producing a slight reduction in scoring efficiency compared to his normal standard.
